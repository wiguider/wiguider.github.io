**Walid Iguider, Ph.D.** is the Chief Technology Officer at [Neural Factory S.R.L.](https://neuralfactory.it/), an AI platform company accelerating Italian manufacturing SMEs through intelligent automation and data-driven decision-making. He is a PhD computer scientist and entrepreneur with **12+ years of experience** building intelligent systems — from algorithmic fairness and recommender systems to autonomous agents and enterprise LLM platforms.

At Neural Factory, Dr. Iguider leads the technical vision, designing a three-tier AI system combining knowledge graphs, conversational analytics, and autonomous agents for manufacturing digitization.

Prior to Neural Factory, Dr. Iguider served as a Senior AI Engineer at [FairMind S.R.L.](https://www.fairmind.ai), where he led R&D of a generative AI platform for software development acceleration, built production-ready LLM systems, and contributed open-source models to HuggingFace. Before that, he was a senior data scientist at [Stackhouse](https://www.stackhouse.it) ([Spindox S.P.A.](https://www.spindox.it) company), designing data-driven marketing AI for [Stellantis](https://www.stellantis.com/) across Europe.

Dr. Iguider holds a bachelor's degree in Mathematics and Computer Science from the [University of Rabat](https://www.um5.ac.ma/), a master's degree in Software Quality from [Sidi Mohamed Ben Abdellah University of Fez](http://www.usmba.ac.ma/), and completed his doctoral program in Computer Science, with a focus on Data Science, at the [University of Cagliari](https://www.unica.it/unica/en/homepage.page).

His experience includes collaborating with national and international companies in **Emirates, France, Italy, Morocco, and Spain.** He is fluent in **Arabic, English, French, and Italian**.
